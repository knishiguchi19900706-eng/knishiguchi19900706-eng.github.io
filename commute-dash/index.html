Dash through the morning rush hour with a single tap

Packed trains, a scramble crossing full of people, crowded sidewalks. Find the gaps in the crowd and beat your coworkers to the office. Your goal: clock in by 9:00:00.

  • Five modes: Full Commute, Scramble Crossing, Race to the Office, Beat Your Coworker! and Commuter Train Rush
  • Climb the career ladder: Newcomer, Senior, Section Chief, Manager, President
  • A Daily Challenge that everyone in the world plays once a day. Share your result as emoji
  • Just tap, hold and swipe. A "Now!" cue and a quick practice run help first-time players
  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no account. Your data stays on your device
