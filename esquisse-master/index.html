エスキスマスターとは

一級建築士 設計製図試験のエスキス（計画の下書き）を、手順どおりに練習するための学習アプリです。 通勤などの短い時間には 1 手順ずつの練習と計算の検算を、まとまった時間には課題を通して解く演習ができます。

About Esquisse Master

A study app for practicing the esquisse (planning sketch) of Japan's first-class architect design exam, step by step. Use short breaks to practice one step at a time and check your calculations, and longer sessions to work through a full assignment.

Pricing

The Reiwa 1 (2019) past exam and more are free. All features are available with a subscription (Standard plan: JPY 3,300 per month or JPY 33,000 per year, tax included), with a 2-week free trial for first-time subscribers. See the Legal Disclosure for details.
